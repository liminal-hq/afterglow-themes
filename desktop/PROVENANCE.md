# Provenance of the Afterglow ReactOS visual style

**Licence:** Apache-2.0 OR MIT, (c) Copyright 2026 Liminal HQ, Scott Morris, the same as the rest of this repository (`LICENSE-APACHE`, `LICENSE-MIT`). That covers the scripts, the INI template, the registry files and the generated `afterglow.msstyles`.

**Clean room.** No Lunar, Lautus, Mizu, Blackshade, Modern or Luna file was copied, traced or recoloured, and none was opened: no bitmap, INI text, section order or comment of another visual style was read. Engine behaviour came from the ReactOS source (read for facts, nothing copied) and from testing in a guest.

## Logon banner

`logon/banner-a.svg`, `logon/bar.svg` and the fallback `logon/banner-b.svg` are original Liminal HQ art, drawn as vectors with their text outlined from DejaVu Sans (the DejaVu font licence allows embedding outlines). No ReactOS bitmap or wordmark was copied, traced or recoloured. `logon/render.py` renders them to the 24-bit BMPs, recorded in `logon/SHA256SUMS`.

## Boot logo

`boot/boot-logo.svg` is original Liminal HQ art, drawn as a vector from the geometry of a spike that drew the same design in palette indices, with its text outlined from Noto Sans (Light and Medium; the font licence allows embedding outlines). No ReactOS bitmap or wordmark was copied, traced or recoloured. `boot/palette.json` is the 16-colour palette, each entry exact in six bits. `boot/render.py` renders the bitmaps, recorded in `boot/SHA256SUMS`.

## Start orb and menu banner

`start/orb.svg` and `start/banner.svg` are original Liminal HQ art, drawn as vectors from the geometry of a spike that drew the same dusk badge and strip in pixels, with the banner's wordmark outlined from Noto Sans Light (the font licence allows embedding outlines). No ReactOS bitmap or wordmark was copied, traced or recoloured. `start/render.py` renders them to the two DIBs, recorded in `start/SHA256SUMS`.

## Palette

The colours are in the `SCHEMES` table in `style/agdraw.py`, one entry per colour scheme, on named slots. They come from four places:

- **Dusk.** The Liminal HQ Afterglow tokens of the Liminal Installer's Studio (`apps/studio/src/theme/tokens.css`): surfaces `#050507`, `#0c0c10`, `#111116`, `#14161d`, `#1b1e28`, `#262a37`, text `#e0e0e0`, `#b4bfce`, `#7c8796`, gold `#ffaa40`, `#e89930`, `#ffd08e`, rose `#f43f5e` and its ramp. Several of them are also in this repository's `scripts/palette.mjs`: `#050507` is `brand.void` and `neutrals.void` 900, `#e0e0e0` is `brand.text`, `#b4bfce` is `brand.textMuted`, `#ffaa40` is `brand.orange` and `#f43f5e` is `brand.red`. The rest (the near-black surface steps, the gold and rose ramps) exist only in the Studio tokens, so the table keeps literal hex values rather than deriving them: the ReactOS style needs exact, hand-picked steps that `palette.mjs`'s nine-step ramps do not contain.
- **Dusk caption ramp.** The seven glow steps of the Quill wallpaper (`#1f2554` to `#7a4562`), the horizon edge `#4a4580`, the ink `#f0a830` and the tile navy `#0a1230`.
- **Ember, Aurora, Graphite.** The owner's design canvas "Quill ReactOS Afterglow Theme Boards": the palette tables in its Caption, Taskbar and Desk parts, used as exact hex values. Two values the canvas does not give were derived in `agdraw.py` (`use_scheme`): the pressed check box outline (the control ramp's first step 60 % of the way to the scheme's night) and the desktop `Background` colour (the first ramp step halfway to night). Only palette data was read; no design or art was copied.
- **The four light schemes.** Written for this style in `agdraw.py`, in the hue family of each dark scheme, and checked for title contrast by `gen.py`.
- **Classic client colours** (212 208 200 and the rest) are ReactOS's own classic scheme values, read from a guest's `HKCU\Control Panel\Colors`.

`gen.py` keeps a contrast gate: title text must reach 4.5:1 on every caption ramp step in every scheme, and it stops the build when one does not.

## What was consulted

- **ReactOS 0.4.16 engine source** (GPL and LGPL code, read for facts about behaviour, nothing copied), tag `0.4.16-release`: `dll/win32/uxtheme` (`msstyles.c`, `draw.c`, `nonclient.c`, `themehooks.c`, `ncscrollbar.c`, `metric.c`, `system.c`, `uxini.c`, `stylemap.c`), `dll/win32/comctl32` (which classes, parts and states each control opens), `base/shell/explorer` (the class names the shell asks for), `dll/cpl/desk` (`theme.c`, `appearance.c`: how Display Properties lists and applies colour schemes), `win32ss/user/ntuser` (`sysparams.c`, `metric.c`: the caption metrics) and `sdk/include/psdk/vssym32.h` (part and state numbers, Microsoft's public names).
- **Microsoft's public visual styles documentation** ("Parts and States", the property names), only through the names in `vssym32.h` and `stylemap.c`; no web page text was used.
- **The Lunar, Lautus, Mizu and Blackshade themes were not opened.**

## Pointers

**Original art.** The 14 pointers are drawn by `pointers/gen-cursors.py` from polygons, circles, rounded rectangles and strokes defined in the script on a 32-unit grid, supersampled 8× with Pillow and reduced to 32 × 32 with 8-bit alpha. Nothing was traced or recoloured, and no Lunar, Mizu, Blackshade or other pointer set was opened or used. The colours are the Dusk palette (navy `#1f2554`, gold `#ffaa40`, rose `#f43f5e`).

**What was measured.** `user32.rc`'s list of resource ids and names was read to know which groups to repoint, and the stock ReactOS pointers in `user32.dll` were measured for bounding boxes and hot spots only, so each Afterglow pointer has the stock pointer's footprint and hot spot at the standard size. None of their pixels are used.

**The patch tool.** `pointers/patch-user32.py` is written for this repository and contains no ReactOS code. It reads a caller-supplied copy of the ReactOS 0.4.16 i386 `user32.dll` (LGPL-2.1), checks its size and SHA-256, and writes a separate file with an extra section. No patched binary is committed or distributed; the sums in `pointers/SHA256SUMS.user32` are a record for checking your own output.

## Icons

**Original art.** The 185 icons are drawn by `icons/gen_e.py`, `icons/gen_v2.py`, `icons/gen_v3.py` and `icons/gen_v4.py` (the 67 added in v2, the 19 of v3 and the 26 of v4, with `gen_e.py`'s own face model and materials) from rectangles, circles, paths and strokes defined in the script on a 48-unit grid (and, for 82 icons, a 16 px frame drawn pixel by pixel: 62 of them in `icons/hint16_v2.py`), with one face model (two tones, a gentle ramp, a lit band, a clipped inner edge, a contact shadow) applied by rule. The folder family comes from `icons/folders_g.py`, which draws the chosen G-M1 folder from the same kind of shapes, and the shortcut arrow (the A1 paper coin, bent 135 degrees) from `icons/arrows.py`. The colours are the Afterglow tokens plus ramp ends, edges and tones derived from them in the scripts. Nothing was traced or recoloured, and no ReactOS, Windows or third-party icon or overlay bitmap was opened or used. The design followed Microsoft Learn's public app icon design and construction guidance and Fluent 2's iconography notes for grid, radii, sizes and light direction; only the guidance was read. The ReactOS 0.4.16 source (resource headers and `.rc` files, `hivecls.inf`, `shortcuts.inf` and the toolbar's image index constants in `browseui/toolsband.cpp`) and the stock 0.4.16 files (`shell32.dll`, `user32.dll`, `stobject.dll`, the Control Panel applets and the programs) were read for icon group ids, toolbar cell indexes, frame sizes and the overlay's footprint, which is where the verified ids in `icons/map.json` come from: only the resource directories were read, and none of their pixels are used.

## Cards

**Original art.** The card back and the two empty-pile marks are drawn by `cards/gen_cards.py` from rectangles, circles, paths and gradients defined in the script, in the colours of the icon set (`icons/gen_e.py`), and rendered by `cards/render.py`; the two review-only backs in `cards/alternatives/` are drawn the same way. The stock `cards.dll` of ReactOS 0.4.16 was read for its bitmap ids, sizes and depth, and its backs were looked at to find the one that carries the ReactOS logo; no pixel of it is used. The felt colour's location in `sol.exe` and `spider.exe` was read from the stock files and from cardlib's source.
